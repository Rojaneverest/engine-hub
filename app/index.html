Engine sound

Up to 60 rpm the sound follows the model: each event plays as the animation reaches it. Press Play, or scrub the timeline to hear them one at a time.

Above 60 rpm you hear a real engine at that speed while the model plays, slowed down and not synced. Compare layouts: the V8’s uneven beat comes from its firing order.

Synthesised live from the model's own timing, not a recording. Press M to switch sound on or off.
